## Durable execution fundamentals

6 entries. [Evidence-Carrying Termination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23623) lets an agent return COMPLETE only with a typed certificate (0 of 288 unsafe completions versus 252 of 288 for a termination-critic baseline, on fully synthetic tasks), [Bounded Loops](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27871) names self-attestation as a class of gate that passes anything, and [BEGIN AI TRANSACTION](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05412) argues that checkpoints give no contract when prompts and tools change while a run is paused. The one measurement of durability's cost is [Temporal under the hood](https://backend.how/posts/temporal-under-the-hood/).

## Determinism vs LLM non-determinism

5 entries. [Vosti](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38981) finds production deterministic inference modes still produce different logits under some execution variations, [Yuan et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09501) measure up to 9% accuracy variation from GPU count, type and batch size, and [Li's LIMBO study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29095) shows the tool contract, not the model, decides exactly-once when the outcome cannot be read back. The design answer is [Controlled Non-Determinism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06255), a deterministic envelope with an exit check.

## Cost economics of agentic pipelines

11 entries. Token price is the wrong number: [What Does a Harness Buy?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04433) finds up to 3x cost differences from the preamble alone, [FrontierHarness](https://runta.com/blog/introducing-frontierharness-eval/) puts cost per completed task from $1.05 to $18.34 on the same tasks, and [Token Reduction Is Not Cost Reduction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12161) cut 38.4% of tool-output tokens and still raised billed cost by 6.8%.
