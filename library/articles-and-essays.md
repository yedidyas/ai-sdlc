## Determinism vs LLM non-determinism

7 entries. The underlying physics: [Thinking Machines](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/defeating-nondeterminism-in-llm-inference/) got 80 unique completions from 1,000 runs at temperature 0 and blames batch-variant kernels rather than the usual concurrency-plus-floating-point story, [SGLang's deterministic mode](https://www.lmsys.org/blog/2025-09-22-sglang-deterministic/) costs roughly 25% to 45% of throughput, and [caching is a more robust tool than seeds](https://www.zansara.dev/posts/2026-03-24-temp-0-llm/). So the practical answer is to record outputs instead of trying to make the model repeat itself.
